Success: at least 40% of the needed yards on 1st down, 60% on 2nd, a first down on 3rd or 4th; a touchdown always counts, a turnover never does. Calls with 8 or more snaps are ranked first, by the low end of their 90% range, so a call needs a good rate and enough snaps to rank high; calls with fewer snaps follow, flagged as small samples. The record gives run direction and pass area, not the full play, and a sack cannot be tied to a pass area, so the dropback total includes sacks.
