# GoBlueBot

Browse every Michigan offensive snap from the 2026 season's official play-by-play, and study the fourth-and-three at Oregon (November 14, 2026) against the plays Michigan had already run.

**Open the app:** https://danielapayne.github.io/GoBlueBot/

## What it does

- Holds the 570 Michigan offensive snaps from the nine games before Oregon. Each game's snaps match its official box-score totals.
- Filters by run or dropback, game and down; searches by player or play text; and has a preset for the target situation (3rd/4th and 1-5).
- Shows how often the filtered snaps converted and how many turnovers they had. These are past results, not predictions.
- Opens each snap's official play text with a link to its source box score.
- Exports a study as JSON.

## What it does not do

The official play-by-play gives the alignment family (usually Shotgun), the direction and the pass area. It does not give the full formation, personnel, assignments or the called play, so the app leaves those blank. It makes no forecasts.
